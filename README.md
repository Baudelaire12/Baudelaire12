## About me

I turn data into decisions. With a background in **statistics and econometrics** and hands-on **machine learning**, I build models that answer concrete questions in **health, finance, agriculture and the environment**, with a focus on African contexts.

On the research side, I work on **trustworthy AI and blockchain**, and I have a keen interest in **digital twins**.

- 📄 **Research paper (2026)**: a blockchain architecture to certify and audit AI-based medical diagnoses (pneumonia), published by the **Africa Centre of Excellence in Blockchain Research**, Young Researchers' Community Program.
- 🥈 **Silver medal**, AirQo African Air Quality Prediction Challenge: PM2.5 prediction from satellite data across 8 African cities.
- ✍️ **40 science outreach articles** on how maths, data and AI transform health, finance and agriculture ([read the blog](https://site-pinel-dahoui.vercel.app/fr/blog)).
